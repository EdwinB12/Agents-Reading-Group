# AGENTS.md

## Conventions

- Keep `references.bib` entries in sync with citations added to `README.md` or the slides.
- Slides are Marp Markdown using the `uos` theme in `slides.css` — build/preview with the Marp CLI or VS Code Marp extension.
- Facts about the OpenAI results are contested and moving; prefer primary sources (repos, papers, official statements) and hedge secondary-reported figures.
- The "Mathpocalypse" title is sarcastic. The session's stance is broadly optimistic: AI progress in maths is good for research and human progress, and the joke gently teases academics' existential dread. Keep the tone light and avoid doom framing, but still present critical views fairly as discussion material.
